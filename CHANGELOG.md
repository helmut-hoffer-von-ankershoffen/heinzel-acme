## [0.1.1](https://github.com/helmut-hoffer-von-ankershoffen/heinzel-acme/releases/tag/v0.1.1) - 2026-10-25

The Acme reference company now includes company, organization, site and team plugins with a weekly-report playbook. A preview-first rehearsal command prepares sample resources in trial accounts; local and Git-based installation of the four-layer stack is verified, while live vendor rehearsal remains unverified.
